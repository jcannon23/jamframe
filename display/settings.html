Client ID

Adds the exact place in the song (lyrics land on the line), what is up next, the playlist name, a heart on liked songs, and the buttons under the preview. Sign-in happens on Spotify's own site; JamFrame never sees your password.

Once: at developer.spotify.com create an app, give it the redirect address , tick Web API, and paste its Client ID here. Connect from the computer JamFrame runs on.
